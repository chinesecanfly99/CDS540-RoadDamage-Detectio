## Candidate Topics｜候选主题

**EN:** Your project should be related to Computer Vision. The following topics are examples; other Computer Vision topics are also welcome.

**中文：** 项目应与计算机视觉相关。以下为示例，也欢迎其他计算机视觉主题。

1. **Image processing and enhancement｜图像处理与增强**  
   EN: Image denoising, deblurring, contrast enhancement, super-resolution, or image restoration.  
   中文：图像去噪、去模糊、对比度增强、超分辨率或图像复原。
2. **Image classification and recognition｜图像分类与识别**  
   EN: Object or scene classification, visual recognition, or visual anomaly detection.  
   中文：物体或场景分类、视觉识别或视觉异常检测。
3. **Object detection and image segmentation｜目标检测与图像分割**  
   EN: Object detection, semantic or instance segmentation, text detection, or medical image segmentation.  
   中文：目标检测、语义分割或实例分割、文本检测或医学图像分割。
4. **Video understanding and analysis｜视频理解与分析**  
   EN: Object tracking, action recognition, motion estimation, or video event detection.  
   中文：目标跟踪、动作识别、运动估计或视频事件检测。
5. **3D computer vision｜三维计算机视觉**  
   EN: Depth estimation, stereo vision, 3D reconstruction, or point cloud processing.  
   中文：深度估计、立体视觉、三维重建或点云处理。
6. **Generative models for visual content｜视觉内容生成模型**  
   EN: Image or video generation, image editing, image-to-image translation, or style transfer.  
   中文：图像或视频生成、图像编辑、图像到图像转换或风格迁移。

## Notes for Selecting Your Topic｜选题注意事项

### Personal Interests｜个人兴趣
**EN:** Choose a visual task that interests your team. For example, a photography-related project could enhance image quality, while a sports-related project could track players or recognize actions in videos.

**中文：** 选择团队感兴趣的视觉任务。例如，摄影相关项目可提升图像质量；体育相关项目可跟踪运动员或识别视频中的动作。

### Course Content and Learning Goals｜课程内容与学习目标
**EN:** Apply concepts covered in CDS540, such as image processing, convolutional neural networks, vision transformers, detection, segmentation, or visual generation. Topics beyond the course content are welcome as long as they are related to Computer Vision.

**中文：** 应用 CDS540 涵盖的概念，例如图像处理、卷积神经网络、视觉 Transformer、检测、分割或视觉生成。课程以外的主题也可以选择，只要与计算机视觉相关。

### Practicality and Feasibility｜实用性与可行性
**EN:** Consider the available data, computing resources, time, and your team's experience. You may use existing libraries and pre-trained models. Choose a manageable task and clearly explain your implementation, experiments, and findings.

**中文：** 考虑可用数据、计算资源、时间和团队经验。可以使用现有程序库及预训练模型。选择工作量可控的任务，并清楚说明实现方法、实验和研究发现。

### Relevance and Impact｜相关性与影响
**EN:** Consider a practical visual problem, such as detecting product defects, analyzing traffic, or improving low-quality images. Define a clear objective and evaluate whether your approach achieves it.

**中文：** 考虑实际视觉问题，例如检测产品缺陷、分析交通状况或改善低质量图像。明确目标，并评估所采用的方法是否达成目标。

### Team Collaboration｜团队协作
**EN:** Choose a topic that reflects the interests and skills of all group members. Divide the work fairly so that each member makes a meaningful contribution.

**中文：** 选择能体现全体成员兴趣与技能的主题。公平分工，确保每位成员都作出有意义的贡献。

## Notes for the Group Project Report｜小组项目报告要求

**EN:** Upload the report to Moodle as a .pdf file. Use Times New Roman, 12-point font, 1.25 line spacing, and 0.5-line spacing after each paragraph. The main text must not exceed 8 pages of A4 paper in portrait orientation. Describe the problem, relevant background, method, data, experiments, results, and limitations. Acknowledge any external code, datasets, pre-trained models, and other sources used.

**中文：** 将报告以 PDF 格式上传至 Moodle。使用 Times New Roman 字体、12 磅字号、1.25 倍行距，段后间距为 0.5 行。正文不超过 8 页纵向 A4 纸。介绍问题、相关背景、方法、数据、实验、结果和局限性。注明所使用的外部代码、数据集、预训练模型及其他资料来源。

## Notes for Group Presentation Slides｜小组汇报幻灯片要求

**EN:** Upload the slides to Moodle as a .ppt, .pptx, or .pdf file. You must use the slides downloaded from Moodle when presenting.

**中文：** 将幻灯片以 .ppt、.pptx 或 .pdf 格式上传至 Moodle。汇报时必须使用从 Moodle 下载的幻灯片。

## Notes for the Group Presentation｜小组汇报要求

**EN:** Each group has 20 minutes in total: 17 minutes for the presentation and 3 minutes for Q&A. The presentation order will be random and announced on Moodle before the presentations begin. Please note: If you exceed the time limit, your presentation will be interrupted.

**中文：** 每组共 20 分钟：17 分钟汇报，3 分钟问答。汇报顺序随机决定，并将在汇报开始前通过 Moodle 公布。注意：超时将被打断。

## Notes for Peer Evaluation｜组员互评要求

**EN:** Individual scores will be based on group performance and peer evaluation. Each student must privately submit an evaluation of the contributions and performance of the other members of their group.

**中文：** 个人成绩根据小组表现和组员互评确定。每位学生必须私下提交对本组其他成员贡献和表现的评价。
